Apex SAT Prep — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 August 2026

Apex SAT Prep ("the app") is an SAT preparation app for students. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app handles.

The short version

Apex SAT Prep collects no personal data. Nothing leaves your device.

What the app stores

Your study progress — practice answers, test scores, accuracy statistics — is saved only in the app's local storage on your own device. This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

If you choose to create an optional in-app profile (first name, last name, email, and an automatically generated username), those details are likewise saved only on your own device. They are never transmitted, uploaded, or shared with us or anyone else.

What the app does NOT do

The app does not transmit any information off your device (the optional profile exists purely in local storage); does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos; does not contain advertising or ad tracking; does not use analytics services; does not require an account or login; and does not connect to the internet to function.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for students, including those under 18. Because the app collects no data at all, no personal information about any user — child or adult — is gathered, stored remotely, or shared.

Purchases

The app is free to download. One optional in-app purchase ("Apex Full Access") unlocks all features permanently; it is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment information; refer to Apple's or Google's privacy policies for how they handle purchase data.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised before that version is released.
